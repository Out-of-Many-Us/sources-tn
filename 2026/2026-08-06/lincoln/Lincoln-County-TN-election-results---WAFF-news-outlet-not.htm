Lincoln County, TN election results

By WAFF 48 Digital Staff
Published: Aug. 6, 2026 at 7:22 PM CDT|Updated: Aug. 7, 2026 at 12:03 AM CDT
Add Us On GoogleAdd as a preferred source on Google

LINCOLN COUNTY, Tenn. (WAFF) - Voting has wrapped up across Lincoln County as polling locations officially closed their doors for the evening!

Local election workers are now beginning the process of counting and finalizing the ballots.

WAFF 48 is keeping a close watch on several highly anticipated, contested local races throughout the county, including matchups for mayor, sheriff, school board, and multiple county commission seats.

Several uncontested positions also appeared on today’s ballot, including roles for Circuit Court Clerk, County Clerk, County Trustee, and Register of Deeds.

Stay with WAFF 48 as we track incoming numbers and deliver updates as they are reported.
